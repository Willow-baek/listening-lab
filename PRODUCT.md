# Listening Lab
React + TypeScript responsive browser-local listening practice app. Korean learner-facing guidance with English training names. No accounts, AI calls, automatic scores, transcript extraction, or cloud storage.
User supplies a YouTube clip after choosing material with ChatGPT. Transcript is first pasted during STEP 2 after saving the unaided STEP 1 notes; no transcript on setup. STEP 2 displays an explicit transcript-waiting state; after paste, the learner starts the study clock explicitly. Its clock does not depend on YouTube being ready or playing. Seven fixed stages: First Listening, Transcript Study, Listening × 10 three times, Difficult Parts, Shadowing. Allocate at least 60 minutes after reserving 30 complete clip plays. For clips over 40 seconds, retain 40 minutes for the four other stages and extend the total. No 90-second cap. Offer whole-clip or two-session mode with visible per-session and combined estimates. Each half preserves all seven stages and thirty repeats with independent notes and assessment; store the pending next part locally, resume it after the first result or from setup, and retain completed history. Official visible YouTube player; no extracted audio. Pause freezes practice time and playback. Preserve local drafts and history.
After stage seven, a final assessment (never stage eight) collects five self-assessments and side-by-side before/after notes. Explicit conservative rules suggest LEVEL UP, KEEP LEVEL, or LEVEL DOWN. First listening never independently lowers difficulty. Ambiguous responses keep level. Save responses, reasons, duration, actual repeat count and reflection. Start next session returns to a new link while retaining history.
Visual brief from supplied product instructions: minimal, calm, focused; meditation timer meets learning notebook. Operate mode. Responsive, keyboard accessible, reduced-motion support.

## Current interaction changes (supersedes earlier completion/reflection details)
Official visible YouTube player is last in the training content; upper controls use its IFrame API. No concealment, clipping or audio extraction. Repeat stages 3–5 hold at ten and need manual Next; stage 6 expiry also holds. Default 3-second inter-repeat gap pauses media and practice clock, adjustable to 0/2/3/5s; pause freezes gap countdown. No bell in this version.
STEP 2 imports published ABC scripts via a constrained server route. A mapped YouTube ID or supplied ABC Learn English URL selects the source; no AI, YouTube scraping or third-party transcript service. Untimed official transcripts are full-video text, explicitly labelled and editable, never overwritten silently. Not exposed before STEP 2.
Comprehension uses five approximate self-reported bands. Old four-point records retain their meaning. Before/After comparison and download UI are removed; initial STEP 1 notes and local history remain.

Copy preference: omit motivational slogans and decorative introductory copy on task/result screens. Result starts directly with completion status and practical metrics/recommendation.
